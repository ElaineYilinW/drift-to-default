\section{Discussion}\label{sec:discussion}
Any autoregressive sampler with finite memory that is ergodic under a fixed conditioning will eventually forget its
initial state, so that video rollouts relax is expected; what we measure is how much is kept and what decides it.
Across nine models the answer is whether the opening stays in the context. Once it leaves a key--value window it is
forgotten, and overlapping frames only delay this; an opening that stays (a re-rotated sink, an anchor, a per-section
prefix, full history) keeps a share whose size depends on the model and that is still there at 180\,s. The larger shares
and returning scenes of sinks at their absolute positions are positional bursts, brief copies of the opening at the distances where the sink draws the most attention. The
default is set by the conditioning, for tone and for scene content, and models differ in which part of the
conditioning carries which attribute: FramePack's image embedding sets the scene but not its brightness. Fresh rollouts
of the two models trained on their own long rollouts barely drift, though they still forget (exploratory).

A high $\alpha$ is not always better. Under a conflicting prompt (a snowy opening, a city prompt), forgetting the
opening is following the prompt, and an anchor that keeps the opening also passes its errors forward (SkyReels
amplifies the shift in 5 of 12 scenes). Persistence is better treated as a property to choose and to report at a stated
horizon, together with the default and its offset; our protocol measures both cheaply and would make long-video models
comparable where a single quality score does not. For world models, a model conditioned on a real frame and a
description drifts toward the description's default unless it re-conditions on the frame. In Self Forcing the
mechanism that keeps the opening's scene is the unstable, positional one: keeping the opening's content while
forgetting its errors is the open problem.

\paragraph{Limitations.} Six of the nine models share the Wan2.1-1.3B architecture and a seventh is a student of its
14B sibling; FramePack and MAGI-1 have other bases. Content relaxation was replicated on FramePack, but the prompt dependence, the recursion
and the decomposition rest on Wan-based models, and the world model collapses after 20--30\,s. Interventions shift global tone or select a scene class (CLIP scores are a
coarse proxy for content); openings are held images; scene content is compared across openings rather than shifted within one; most configurations use one seed per scene (the Self Forcing sink results on tone and content replicate with three more); Holm correction is
applied within studies only; the correction was not compared with Pathwise test-time correction or FreqForcing.
