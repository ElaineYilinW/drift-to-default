# Preregistration 39: without a given opening, does a sink held at D\* give less color drift and less loss of imaging quality than the re-aligned sink? (VBench-style measures, 4 models + Rolling Forcing as reference) (2026-11-02, frozen before generation)

## Background
- Studies 37 and 38 measure "how much of the opening is kept". Reviewers will ask whether this is useful for long-video
  generation in general. This study uses 60-s videos generated from text (the first chunk is generated by the model
  itself and serves as the sink), evaluates quality per segment following VBench, and compares 4 sink settings.
- Preregistration 30: at 60 s, Self Forcing without a sink ends 16.4 L\* from the start, with the re-aligned sink 9.6,
  and with the sink at its original positions 3.8 (but with bursts).
- **Data status:** no video of this study had been generated when it was frozen.

## Design
- **Prompts:** the 40 of `prompts/main.jsonl` (20 MovieGen, 20 driving). **Seed:** 13 (preregistrations 30–36 used
  10–12). **Duration:** 60 s (243 latent frames). No opening is given (`--fresh`). Video files are written.
- **Models and arms:** the same 4 models as study 38 (only those with a D\* take part), 4 arms per model:
  - s0: no sink;
  - rr: sink 3 held at the re-aligned distance (20, LongLive 11);
  - held: sink 3 held at D\*;
  - abs: sink 3 at its original positions.
- **Reference:** Rolling Forcing (released settings, `rf_gen_long.py`).
- **Execution:** one generation task per model × prompt (arch_guard), made by `make_s39_tasks.py` (Self Forcing,
  LongLive and Rolling Forcing now; CausVid and GAN after study 37). Then one evaluation task per model × prompt
  (`vbench_lite.py`).

## Measures (`vbench_lite.py`, frozen)
- Each video, with the first chunk (frames 0–8) removed, is cut into 10-s segments; segment k = frames
  [9 + 160k, 9 + 160(k + 1)). Per segment:
  - **imaging quality (imaging):** MUSIQ (SPAQ weights), 1 frame in every 8, long side resized to 512 (as in VBench);
  - **aesthetic quality (aesthetic):** LAION aesthetic predictor v1 (a linear head on CLIP ViT-L/14 embeddings), 1
    frame in every 8, divided by 10;
  - **subject consistency (subject):** DINO ViT-B/16 CLS features, 1 frame in every 4, averaged within the segment
    with VBench's formula (cos(f_i, f_1) + cos(f_i, f_{i−1})) / 2;
  - **background consistency (background):** the same formula, with CLIP ViT-B/32;
  - **motion (flow):** RAFT-large optical flow, 1 frame in every 2 (8 fps), half resolution, the mean of the largest 5%
    of magnitudes in each pair (the score of VBench's dynamic degree before thresholding).
- **Color offset:** |mean L\* over [50, 60) s − mean L\* over [0, 5) s| (the per-frame L\* recorded by the generator).
- **Change in imaging quality:** imaging quality of segment 5 − imaging quality of segment 0.

## Hypotheses (`analyze_s39.py`, frozen; Holm correction over all participating models together, at most 8 tests)
- **B1_m held drifts less in color:** offset(rr) − offset(held) > 0.
- **B2_m held loses less imaging quality:** change in imaging quality(held) − change in imaging quality(rr) > 0.
- **Tests:** two-sided one-sample t-tests over prompts, supported if Holm p < 0.05 and the estimate > 0; 95% CIs by
  prompt bootstrap (10,000 draws).

## Secondary analyses
- per model and arm (and Rolling Forcing): the mean and change of each dimension in segments 0 and 5, the color
  offset, motion and flow over [50, 60) s, frozen share;
- the same comparisons of held against s0 and against abs, and flow;
- results for the driving and MovieGen prompts separately.

## Predictions
- **B1:** supported for Self Forcing (rr about 9.6, held about 5–7 L\*); may be supported for LongLive but with a small
  difference (its offset is small to begin with); GAN and CausVid in the same direction.
- **B2:** uncertain. The sink keeps the picture closer to the start, which may reduce the loss of imaging quality; but
  it may also blur detail. The difference is expected to be small (0–0.03).
- **Rules for interpretation:**
  - if B1 is supported but B2 is not: the held distance reduces color drift but does not improve VBench imaging
    quality. The paper states this as it is.
  - if flow is clearly lower than for rr (secondary analysis): part of held's stability comes from less motion, and
    this has to be reported.
  - abs may be as good as held on these measures, or better; its problem is the bursts, which study 38 measures.
