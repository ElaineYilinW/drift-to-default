# Preregistration 24: does an opening that stays in the context keep a steady share over 180 s? (Self Forcing, Rolling Forcing, LongLive, 180 s) (2026-10-28, frozen before generation)

*English translation; the Chinese file `../24_long_horizon.md` is the frozen, authoritative version.*

## Background
- The paper divides the models into two classes:
  - when the opening leaves the context (it is only in the sliding window, or is carried forward only through overlapping frames), it is forgotten;
  - when the opening stays in the context (re-rotated sink, Rolling Forcing's anchor, FramePack's prefix, MAGI-1's full history), a share of it is kept.
- For the second class, "kept" has so far been seen only up to 60 s, and the curves are still falling:
  - Self Forcing's re-rotated sink 3: 0.30, 0.26, 0.24, 0.23 at 30, 40, 50, 60 s (preregistration 16);
  - Rolling Forcing: from 0.70 to 0.42 over 10–60 s (preregistration 10).
- A review comment pointed out that "keeps a steady share" is a statement about the asymptote, and that 60-s data cannot distinguish "a steady share" from "slower forgetting".
- This study generates the same 24 scenes to 180 s and tests three things:
  - whether these shares are still above 0 at 180 s;
  - whether they stay unchanged from 60 s to 180 s;
  - whether the opening stays forgotten without a sink.

## Design
- **Scenes:** the 24 scenes of preregistration 06 (rx00–rx11, gx00–gx11), generic prompt (`prompts/relax.jsonl`).
- **Length:** 180 s (723 latent frames, 2889 frames, 16 fps).
- **Openings:** L\* shifted by −16 / +16 (versions m16, p16), as in preregistrations 06 and 10.
- **Seed:** 0.
- **Arms:**
  - sf180_s0: Self Forcing DMD, no sink (`hold_gen_long_rr.py --sink 0`);
  - sf180_s3rr: Self Forcing DMD, sink 3, re-rotated (`--sink 3 --rerotate`);
  - sf180_s3: Self Forcing DMD, sink 3, original positions (`--sink 3`);
  - rf180: Rolling Forcing (anchor, realigned positions; `rf_gen_long.py`);
  - ll180_s3: LongLive, sink 3 (`ll_hold_gen_long.py --sink 3`).
- **Fresh rollouts:** 4 seeds (0–3) per arm, without an opening (`--fresh`), for the attractor offset (secondary analysis).
- **Execution:**
  - One task per scene × arm, with both versions; arch_guard keeps a scene's pair of versions on one GPU architecture.
  - Any GPU with ≥ 40 GB and bf16 support.
  - Video files are written only for rx00 and gx00; all statistics are computed from the frames.
  - The task list is generated by `make_s24_tasks.py` (`jobs/s24.jsonl`, 125 tasks).
  - 24 × 5 × 2 + 20 = 260 videos in total.

## Metric
- open: mean L\* of frames 1–8.
- end(T): mean L\* over [T − 10, T) s after the opening.
- α(T) = (end_p16 − end_m16) / (open_p16 − open_m16); undefined if open_p16 − open_m16 < 4 L\*.
- No collapse exclusion (as in preregistrations 10, 20 and 23).

## Hypotheses (`analyze_s24.py`, frozen; Holm over 5 tests; T = 180 s)
- **L1, the re-rotated sink still keeps a share at 180 s:** α(sf180_s3rr, 180) > 0.
- **L2, Rolling Forcing still keeps a share at 180 s:** α(rf180, 180) > 0.
- **L3, the re-rotated sink's share is steady:** α(sf180_s3rr, 180) − α(sf180_s3rr, 60) within ±0.10.
- **L4, Rolling Forcing's share is steady:** α(rf180, 180) − α(rf180, 60) within ±0.10.
- **L5, without a sink the opening stays forgotten:** α(sf180_s0, 180) within ±0.10.
- **Tests:**
  - L1, L2: two-sided one-sample t-test over scenes; supported if Holm p < 0.05 and the estimate lies on the predicted side;
  - L3–L5: equivalence test, one-sided t-tests against the two bounds (TOST), p is the larger one; supported if Holm p < 0.05;
  - 95% CIs by scene bootstrap (10,000 draws); for the equivalence tests the 90% t interval is also reported.
- **Bound:** 0.10, the same as preregistration 23's definition of "forgets completely", about half the share kept by the re-rotated sink at 60 s.

## Secondary analyses
- L1–L5 over the [160, 170) s window.
- Per arm: α at T = 10, 20, …, 180 s (10-s windows) and 1-s curves.
- A fit of α(t) = α∞ + (α0 − α∞)·exp(−t/τ) to each arm's mean 10-s curve (T ≥ 10), with a scene-bootstrap interval for α∞ (2,000 draws).
- α(sf180_s3, 180) − α(sf180_s3rr, 180), and α(ll180_s3, 180).
- For the sinks at their original positions (sf180_s3, ll180_s3), the times of jumps above 0.15 in the 1-s curves (do the bursts continue after 60 s? compared with the positional prediction of preregistration 25).
- Collapse counts (number of videos with a 1-s mean L\* below 3 or above 97).
- Attractor offset of the fresh rollouts: end(T) − mean L\* of the first second, T = 60, 120, 180.

## Predictions
- **L1 holds:** at 60 s the re-rotated sink keeps about 0.2, and the curve is flattening.
- **L2 holds:** Rolling Forcing is at 0.42 at 60 s; at the current rate of decline it is still above 0 at 180 s.
- **L3:** tends to hold (extrapolating the exponential fit, a decline of about 0.03 from 60 to 180 s), but uncertain.
- **L4:** tends not to hold: before 60 s Rolling Forcing declines by about 0.04 every 10 s.
- **L5 holds.**
- **Interpretation rules:**
  - If L3 does not hold and the share keeps falling, the paper's "an opening that stays in the context keeps a steady share" is rewritten as "is forgotten more slowly", and the distinction between the two classes becomes one of rate.
  - If L1 does not hold, the re-rotated sink does not differ from no sink over long horizons.
