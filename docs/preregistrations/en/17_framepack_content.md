# Preregistration 17: which of FramePack's routes carries scene content? (2026-10-25, frozen before generation)

*English translation; the Chinese file `../17_framepack_content.md` is the frozen, authoritative version.*

## Background
- Preregistration 11 showed that FramePack (HunyuanVideo architecture, the only non-Wan base) keeps 0.68 of the opening's brightness at 20 s; the opening enters the model through two routes:
  - **context route:** the opening's VAE latent is a clean prefix of every section;
  - **embedding route:** the opening's SigLIP image embedding is a global condition of every section.
  Of these, a brightness shift sent only through the embedding route has almost no effect (α = 0.01).
- But SigLIP is a semantic encoder: it may not carry tone, yet carry the scene class. If so, "the conditioning sets the default" splits by attribute on FramePack: the embedding route sets the default of content, not the default of tone.
- The content study of preregistration 09 used only Wan-architecture models. This study extends it to FramePack and separates the two routes.

## Design
- **Openings:** the 24 openings of preregistration 09 (6 classes × 4).
- **City images:**
  - the candidates are, in order, the generic-prompt videos generated from scratch by Self Forcing DMD in preregistration 09: fresh_sf60_s3 seeds 0–7, then fresh_sf60_s0 seeds 0–7;
  - frame 64 (4 s) is taken and classified with CLIP ViT-L/14 among the 7 labels;
  - in order, the first 8 frames with "top-1 = city and probability ≥ 0.5" are taken and named ctcity0–7 (`fpc_city_openings.py`).
  - Opening ct{class}{k} is paired with ctcity{k} (k = 0–3).
- **Conditions** (FramePack-F1, generic city prompt, 20 s, seed 0, generation script `fp_gen_c.py`):
  - both: context = opening, embedding = opening;
  - ctx: context = opening, embedding = the paired city image;
  - emb: context = the paired city image, embedding = opening;
  - alt: context = embedding = city image (8 city starts, giving the default).
- **Execution:**
  - both, ctx and emb of one opening are in the same task (arch_guard);
  - all tasks are pinned to the Ada group (L40S, RTX 6000 Ada);
  - task lists are generated by `make_fpc_tasks.py`: a selects the city images, b generates (after a; its content is fully determined by the frozen scripts and the selected images), c runs CLIP (`clip_probe.py`).
  - 24 × 3 + 8 = 80 videos in total.
- **Trial run of the selection script:** before freezing, `fpc_city_openings.py` was run once in a temporary directory, only to check the code. It reads only existing videos of preregistration 09 and involves none of this study's results.

## Metrics (as preregistration 09, frame rate 30)
- Frame-level class score z = CLIP logit scale × (cos(frame, opening's class label) − cos(frame, city label)); frame f corresponds to (f − 9)/30 s after the opening.
- s[t0, t1): mean z within that window.
- s_open: mean z over frames 1–8 (i.e. the opening image) of the opening's both video. The videos of the emb condition start with the city image, so all three conditions use this value.
- μ[t0, t1): mean score of the 8 city starts in the same window.
- κ = (s − μ) / (s_open − μ); undefined when s_open − μ < 2.

## Hypotheses (`analyze_fpc.py`, frozen; Holm correction, 4 tests in total; window [15, 20) s)
- **H1 FramePack's content relaxes:** s(both) − s_open < 0.
- **H2 the context prefix carries content:** κ(ctx) > 0.
- **H3 the SigLIP embedding carries content:** κ(emb) > 0.
- **H4 the embedding adds content persistence:** κ(both) − κ(ctx) > 0.
- **Test method:**
  - two-sided one-sample t-tests over openings;
  - supported if Holm p < 0.05 and the estimate is on the predicted side;
  - 95% CIs by bootstrap (openings resampled within class, city starts resampled as well, 10,000 draws).

## Secondary analyses
- κ curves of each condition (windows [T−5, T), T = 5..20);
- additivity κ(ctx) + κ(emb) − κ(both);
- H2–H4 on tone-normalised embeddings;
- κ per class;
- top-1 accuracy of the opening's class among the 7 labels within [15, 20) s;
- κ(both, 20 s) side by side with Self Forcing DMD's κ at 20 s in preregistration 09 (descriptive).

## Predictions
- **H1 holds:** content relaxes to some degree, but κ(both) is high (about 0.6–0.8).
- **H2 holds:** κ(ctx) about 0.4–0.6.
- **H3 holds:** κ(emb) about 0.2–0.4. This is the opposite of tone: in preregistration 11 the embedding route had no effect on tone.
- **H4 holds.**
