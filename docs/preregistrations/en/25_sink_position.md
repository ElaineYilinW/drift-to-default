# Preregistration 25: do the sink's bursts follow the sink's rotary distance? Does a sink held at a probe peak distance keep more than one held at a trough? (Self Forcing, 60 s) (2026-10-28, frozen before generation)

*English translation; the Chinese file `../25_sink_position.md` is the frozen, authoritative version.*

## Background
- With the sink at its original (absolute) rotary positions, persistence jumps suddenly near 17, 33 and 51 s ("bursts"), and the opening's scene content also returns at these times:
  - this holds for 5 Self Forcing checkpoints and for LongLive (preregistrations 10, 16, 21);
  - it never occurs in the configurations with a re-rotated sink or without a sink.
- Proposed mechanism: as generation proceeds, the sink's keys lie ever further from the current chunk; at some query–sink distances the model gives the sink much of its attention, and the opening is thereby re-injected.
- **Exploratory attention probe** (`jobs/run_sinkprobe.sh`, `analyze_sinkprobe.py`; not part of any preregistration):
  - Setup: 4 unshifted openings (rx00, rx01, gx00, gx01), sink 3, original positions; 20 chunks per opening (starts 12, 24, …, 240), 128 random queries per chunk, 30 layers, 5 attention calls (4 denoising steps + 1 context pass).
  - Measure: for each distance D in a grid (3–300 latent frames), how much attention the queries would give the sink if it were placed at distance D (sink vs window logsumexp).
  - Result (`sinkprobe_out.json`): the sink's share has sharp peaks at D = 71, 134, 203, 266 (0.109, 0.136, 0.150, 0.098; median over 3 ≤ D ≤ 300: 0.073), each peak about 4 latent frames wide; each peak is preceded by a trough (e.g. 0.052 at D = 187, 0.055 at D = 123).
  - In all 4 openings and all chunks the peaks sit at exactly the same distances, so this is a property of the model's positional encoding, unrelated to content.
  - At the original positions, the chunks' natural distance reaches 71, 134 and 203 at 16.5, 32.25 and 49.5 s after the opening, exactly the observed burst times.
- **Consistency check:** the patched Self-Forcing-b with no switch on gives output bit-identical to preregistration 10 (rx00, sink 3, m16, A40).
- **Design check (with existing data of preregistration 10):** with the burst windows defined below, the burst contrast is +0.33 [0.29, 0.38] for sink 3 at its original positions and −0.04 for the re-rotated sink.
- This study intervenes on the sink's position to test two things:
  - that the bursts are set by the distance, not by the time elapsed since the opening or by content;
  - whether a sink held at a peak distance keeps more than one held at a trough or next to the window.

## Design
- **Scenes:** the 24 scenes of preregistration 06 (rx00–rx11, gx00–gx11), generic prompt (`prompts/relax.jsonl`).
- **Model:** Self Forcing DMD, sink 3, window 21, 60 s (243 latent frames).
  - Generator `hold_gen_sinkpos.py`, run in Self-Forcing-b (only `wan/modules/causal_model.py` is a real file, patched by `jobs/patch_sf_sinkpos.py`).
  - The patch rotates only a copy of the sink keys (the cache keeps the original keys), as in the re-rotation of `patch_sf.py`.
- **Openings:** L\* shifted by −16 / +16 (versions m16, p16), as in preregistrations 06 and 10.
- **Seed:** 0.
- **Arms:**
  - sp_abs: original positions (no switch);
  - sp_off18: the sink moved 18 latent frames further back (`--sink_mode off:18`); distance = natural distance + 18;
  - sp_off36: moved 36 latent frames further back (`off:36`);
  - sp_d203: the sink held at distance 203 (a probe peak, `dist:203`);
  - sp_d187: held at distance 187 (the trough before that peak, `dist:187`);
  - sp_d20: held at distance 20 (right next to the window, equivalent to re-rotation, `dist:20`).
- **Execution:**
  - One task per scene, with all arms and versions (12 videos); arch_guard keeps all of a scene's outputs on one GPU architecture.
  - Any GPU with ≥ 40 GB and bf16 support.
  - Video files are written only for rx00 and gx00.
  - The task list is generated by `make_s25_tasks.py` (`jobs/s25.jsonl`, 24 tasks).
  - 24 × 6 × 2 = 288 videos in total.
- The generator also records two per-frame quantities in the npz: motion (mean absolute grey difference from the previous frame, 0–100) and open_diff (mean absolute grey difference from frame 8, the last frame of the opening).

## Metric
- α[t0, t1) = (end_p16 − end_m16) / (open_p16 − open_m16):
  - open is the mean L\* of frames 1–8;
  - end is the mean L\* over seconds [t0, t1) after the opening;
  - undefined if open_p16 − open_m16 < 4 L\*.
- No collapse exclusion.
- **Burst onsets:**
  - A chunk starting at latent frame c is at distance c + 2 + δ from sink frame 0 (δ is the number of frames by which the sink is moved further back: 0, 18, 36); the chunk starts (c − 3)/4 s after the opening.
  - So the bursts start at τ_k(δ) = (P_k − 5 − δ)/4 s, P = 71, 134, 203, 266.
  - Burst window W_k = [τ_k + 0.5, τ_k + 2.5) s; only windows within [5, 60) s are kept:
    - δ = 0: 16.5, 32.25, 49.5 s;
    - δ = 18: 12, 27.75, 45 s;
    - δ = 36: 7.5, 23.25, 40.5, 56.25 s.
  - A_X(δ): the mean α of arm X over all windows of δ (one value per scene).

## Hypotheses (`analyze_s25.py`, frozen; Holm over 4 tests)
- **H1, the bursts follow an 18-frame move:** [A_off18(18) − A_off18(0)] − [A_abs(18) − A_abs(0)] > 0.
- **H2, the bursts follow a 36-frame move:** [A_off36(36) − A_off36(0)] − [A_abs(36) − A_abs(0)] > 0.
- **H3, a peak distance keeps more than a trough:** α(sp_d203, [50, 60)) − α(sp_d187, [50, 60)) > 0.
- **H4, a peak distance keeps more than distance 20:** α(sp_d203, [50, 60)) − α(sp_d20, [50, 60)) > 0.
- **Tests:**
  - two-sided one-sample t-test over scenes;
  - supported if Holm p < 0.05 and the estimate > 0;
  - 95% CIs by scene bootstrap (10,000 draws).
- If the bursts were set only by time (or by the change of content after the opening), the two brackets of H1 and H2 would be equal and the estimates 0.

## Secondary analyses
- H3 and H4 over the [40, 50) s window.
- Each arm's contrast at its own burst onsets: mean_k [α(W_k) − α([τ_k − 2.5, τ_k − 0.5))];
  - sp_abs, sp_off18 and sp_off36 with their own δ;
  - sp_d203, sp_d187 and sp_d20 with the onsets of δ = 0.
- Per arm, the times of jumps above 0.15 in the 1-s curve, against the predicted onsets.
- Per arm: α at T = 10, 20, …, 60 s (10-s windows) and 1-s curves.
- Probe check: over t = 6…59 s, the correlation between the first differences of the mean 1-s α curves of sp_abs, sp_off18 and sp_off36 and the first differences of the sink share that the probe predicts for δ = 0, 18, 36.
- Per arm, mean motion and open_diff over [50, 60) s (both versions counted): does holding the sink at a peak distance make the video freeze or copy the opening?

## Predictions
- **H1, H2 hold:** the bursts come about 4.5 s and 9 s earlier.
- **H3 holds:** at distance 203 the sink receives about 3 times the attention it receives at 187.
  - The size is uncertain: giving the sink attention continuously may also push the video toward copying the opening (see motion and open_diff).
- **H4 holds.**
- **Interpretation rules:**
  - If H1 and H2 do not hold (the bursts stay at their original times), the bursts are not set by the distance, and the positional account in the paper is withdrawn.
  - If H1 and H2 hold but H3 does not: the burst times are set by the distance, but parking the sink at a peak distance does not keep more continuously. A burst is a transient response to the distance passing a peak.
  - If H3 and H4 hold: placing the sink at a peak distance is a training-free way to keep the opening continuously. Whether it has a cost is shown by motion and open_diff.
