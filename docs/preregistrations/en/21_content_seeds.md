# Preregistration 21: do the Self Forcing content results hold with new seeds? (2026-10-27, frozen before generation)

*English translation; the Chinese file `../21_content_seeds.md` is the frozen, authoritative version.*

## Background
- The paper's sink conclusions on scene content (snow, desert, forest, etc.) all come from one seed per opening (seed 0):
  - preregistration 09: sink 3 keeps about 0.6 of the content at 60 s, no sink about 0.14;
  - preregistration 16 A:
    - the re-rotated sink drops to about 0.25;
    - the content "rise" at 50–55 s appears only with the sink at its original positions;
    - the difference between the re-rotated sink and no sink is +0.10 (95% CI [−0.00, 0.21]), not significant.
- Preregistration 20 has replicated the sink conclusions on tone, and found that the values of single videos vary mainly with the seed. So the content conclusions also need a multi-seed test.
- This study redoes the three conditions of preregistration 16 A with three new seeds (1, 2, 3). Each opening takes the mean of three seeds, which has less noise. This also gives a stronger test of "whether the re-rotated sink keeps content".

## Design
- **Openings:** the 24 openings of preregistration 09 (6 classes × 4), generic city prompt (arm mis).
- **Model:** Self Forcing DMD, 60 s (243 latent frames), `hold_gen_long_rr.py` (as in preregistration 16).
- **Conditions:**
  - sf60_s3: sink 3, original positions;
  - sf60_s3rr: sink 3, re-rotated;
  - sf60_s0: no sink.
- **Seeds:** 1, 2, 3.
- **Default:** for each condition, 8 new fresh starts of the generic prompt are generated (seeds 8–15) to estimate μ (earlier fresh videos are not reused).
- **Execution:**
  - one task per opening, containing the three conditions and three seeds (arch_guard checks that all outputs of the same opening come from the same architecture);
  - one task each for the fresh starts of each condition;
  - any GPU model (≥ 40 GB, bf16 support);
  - task lists generated by `make_s21_tasks.py` (`jobs/s21_a.jsonl`; embeddings `jobs/s21_e.jsonl`).
  - 24 × 3 × 3 + 3 × 8 = 240 videos in total.
- **Embeddings:** the primary analysis uses CLIP ViT-L/14 (as in preregistrations 09, 16, `clip_probe.py`). SigLIP so400m embeddings (`siglip_probe.py`) are also computed, for secondary analyses only.

## Metrics (as in preregistration 16 A)
- Class score: z = scale × (cos(frame, class label) − cos(frame, city label)).
- Window score: s[t0, t1) is the mean z of the frames within [t0, t1) s after the opening (frame f corresponds to (f − 9)/16 s); s_open is the mean over frames 1–8.
- Default: μ[t0, t1) is the mean of the same class score in the same window over the fresh starts.
- Content persistence: κ = (s − μ)/(s_open − μ); undefined when s_open − μ < 2.
- Value of an opening: the mean of κ over all seeds of that opening.
- Jump contrast: R = κ[50, 55) − κ[45, 50).

## Hypotheses (`analyze_s21.py`, frozen; Holm correction, 4 tests in total; window [55, 60))
- **H1 the sink keeps content:** κ(sf60_s3) − κ(sf60_s0) > 0.
- **H2 re-rotation lowers what is kept:** κ(sf60_s3rr) − κ(sf60_s3) < 0.
- **H3 the re-rotated sink still keeps content:** κ(sf60_s3rr) − κ(sf60_s0) > 0.
- **H4 the late rise of content comes from position:** R(sf60_s3rr) − R(sf60_s3) < 0.
- **Test method:**
  - two-sided one-sample t-tests over openings;
  - supported if Holm p < 0.05 and the estimate is on the predicted side;
  - 95% CIs by bootstrap (10,000 draws, openings resampled within class, fresh seeds resampled as well).

## Secondary analyses
- H1–H4 redone on tone-removed embeddings (emb_tn);
- H1–H4 estimates for each seed separately;
- κ curves of each condition (windows [T − 5, T), T = 5…60);
- H1–H4 after pooling with seed 0 of preregistration 16 (each with μ estimated from its own fresh starts);
- variance decomposition of κ[55, 60): between openings and between seeds (one-way random-effects ICC);
- H1–H4 redone with SigLIP embeddings (encoder robustness).

## Predictions
- **H1 holds:** about +0.4.
- **H2 holds:** about −0.3.
- **H3:** about +0.1. The seed-0 result was close to the significance boundary, so whether it holds here is uncertain; if it does not hold, the paper's statement remains "no detectable content".
- **H4 holds:** about −0.3.
