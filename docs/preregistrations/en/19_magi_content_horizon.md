# Preregistration 19: scene content on MAGI-1, and the released window at 60 s (2026-10-27, frozen before generation)

*English translation; the Chinese file `../19_magi_content_horizon.md` is the frozen, authoritative version.*

## Background
- Preregistration 18 showed, on MAGI-1, a non-Wan architecture:
  - the released attention window (mgw, sees at most the 4 previous chunks) forgets the opening's brightness: α falls from 0.87 at 5 s to 0.34 at 30 s, and has not reached a plateau by 30 s;
  - full history (mgf) stays at about 0.65 from 10 to 30 s.
- Two questions remain unanswered:
  - **Content:** relaxation of scene content (preregistration 09) has only been measured on Wan-architecture models; it has been measured on FramePack (preregistration 17), not on MAGI-1.
    - Does content also relax on MAGI-1?
    - Does full history also keep more content?
    - Is the content default also set by the prompt?
  - **Horizon:** does the released window keep forgetting after 30 s? The paper's claim is that "models relying only on the KV cache forget the opening completely".
- **Trial run** (submitted before freezing; rx00, not a scene of this study, unshifted, 60 s, A40): one video each for mgw and mgf, only to check whether 60 s runs, and its memory and runtime.
  - They enter no analysis;
  - the mgf 60-s result is only used to decide whether a separate study is done later.

## Design
- **Generation:** `magi_gen.py` (as in preregistration 18; MAGI-1 4.5B distilled version, 432×768, seed 0).
- **GPU:** all tasks are pinned to A40 (`gpu_only`); each task runs arch_guard at its start to check that all outputs of the same opening or scene come from the same architecture.
- **A Content (30 s):**
  - Openings: the 24 openings of preregistration 09 (6 classes × 4).
  - Three conditions per opening:
    - mis_mgw: generic city prompt, released window;
    - mis_mgf: generic city prompt, full history;
    - mat_mgw: the prompt matched to the opening's class (the mat prompts of preregistration 09), released window.
  - Generation from scratch: text-to-video with the generic city prompt, seeds 0–7 each for mgw and mgf.
  - One task per opening × condition; one task per from-scratch video.
  - 72 + 16 = 88 videos in total.
- **B Horizon (60 s):**
  - the 12 scenes of preregistration 08, openings L\* −16 / +16, generic prompt, released window (mgw60);
  - one task per scene × version, 24 videos in total.
- **CLIP:** embeddings of all videos of A are computed with the frozen `clip_probe.py` (the 7 labels of preregistration 09).
- Task lists are generated by `make_magi19_tasks.py` (`jobs/magi19_a.jsonl`, `jobs/magi19_c.jsonl`).

## Metrics
- **Content** (as preregistration 09, frame rate 24):
  - in videos with an opening, frame f corresponds to (f − 9)/24 s after the opening; in from-scratch videos, frame f corresponds to f/24 s.
  - Frame-level class score z = CLIP logit scale × (cos(frame, opening's class label) − cos(frame, city label)).
  - s[t0, t1): mean z within the window; s_open: mean z over frames 1–8 of the same video.
  - μ[t0, t1): mean score of that class in the same window over the from-scratch videos of the same window setting (mgw or mgf).
  - κ = (s − μ) / (s_open − μ); undefined when s_open − μ < 2.
- **Brightness** (as preregistration 18):
  - α(T) = (p16 end − m16 end) / (p16 opening − m16 opening);
  - the opening is the mean L\* of frames 1–8, the end is the mean L\* over [T − 10, T) s.
- **Collapse rule (B):** if either version has any 1-s mean L\* below 3 or above 97 within [0, 60) s, the scene is excluded.

## Hypotheses (`analyze_magi19.py`, frozen; Holm correction, 4 tests in total; window for A W = [25, 30) s)
- **A1 content relaxes under the released window:** s(mis_mgw, W) − s_open < 0.
- **A2 full history keeps more content:** κ(mis_mgf, W) − κ(mis_mgw, W) > 0.
- **A3 the prompt sets the content default:** s(mat_mgw, W) − s(mis_mgw, W) > 0.
- **B1 the released window keeps forgetting:** α(mgw60, 60) − α(mgw60, 30) < 0.
- **Test method:**
  - two-sided one-sample t-tests over openings (A) or scenes (B);
  - supported if Holm p < 0.05 and the estimate is on the predicted side;
  - 95% CIs by bootstrap: for A, openings resampled within class, from-scratch videos resampled as well; for B, scenes resampled; 10,000 draws each.

## Secondary analyses
- κ curves of mis_mgw and mis_mgf (windows [T−5, T), T = 5..30);
- κ(mis_mgf, W) and κ(mis_mgw, W) compared with 0;
- A1–A3 on tone-normalised embeddings;
- κ per class;
- top-1 accuracy of the opening's class among the 7 labels within W (per condition);
- α(mgw60, T) at T = 10, 20, 30, 40, 50, 60, and α(mgw60, 60) compared with 0;
- freezing (as preregistration 18: mean absolute frame-to-frame change of mean L\* within [20, 30) s below 0.01; for B, [50, 60) s is checked as well):
  - frozen videos are reported per condition;
  - A1–A3 and B1 are redone after excluding frozen videos;
- attractor offset of the from-scratch videos: mean L\* over [20, 30) s minus over [0, 2) s.

## Predictions
- **A1 holds.**
- **A2 holds:** as for brightness, full history keeps more.
- **A3 holds.**
- **B1 holds:** α(mgw60, 60) about 0.1, possibly not detectably different from 0.
