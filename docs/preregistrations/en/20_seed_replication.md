# Preregistration 20: do the Self Forcing sink results hold with new seeds? (2026-10-27, frozen before generation)

*English translation; the Chinese file `../20_seed_replication.md` is the frozen, authoritative version.*

## Background
- The paper's sink conclusions for Self Forcing all come from one seed per scene (seed 0):
  - preregistrations 07, 10: sink 3 keeps about 0.6 at 60 s, no sink about 0.05;
  - preregistration 10: after re-rotation the sink drops to 0.16;
  - preregistration 16: re-rotated sink 3 and sink 6 are both about 0.22.
- Two points have not yet been tested:
  - **Seeds:** are the conclusions only a special case of seed 0? How much of the variation is between scenes and how much between seeds?
  - **Does the re-rotated sink still keep tone:** the paper's new claim is that "a sink at normal positions keeps about 0.2 of the tone". But neither preregistration 10 nor 16 compared the re-rotated sink directly with no sink.
- This study redoes these three conditions with three new seeds (1, 2, 3).

## Design
- **Scenes:** the 24 scenes of preregistration 06 (rx00–rx11, gx00–gx11), openings L\* −16 / +16, generic prompt.
- **Model:** Self Forcing DMD, 60 s (243 latent frames), `hold_gen_long_rr.py` (as in preregistrations 10, 16).
- **Conditions:**
  - sf60_s3: sink 3, original positions;
  - sf60_s0: no sink;
  - sf60_s3rr: sink 3, re-rotated.
- **Seeds:** 1, 2, 3.
- **Execution:**
  - one task per scene, containing all conditions, seeds and versions (arch_guard checks that all outputs of the scene come from the same architecture);
  - any GPU model (≥ 40 GB, bf16 support);
  - task list generated by `make_s20_tasks.py` (`jobs/s20.jsonl`).
  - 24 × 3 × 3 × 2 = 432 videos in total.

## Metrics
- For each scene, seed and condition:
  - α(T) = (p16 end − m16 end) / (p16 opening − m16 opening);
  - the opening is the mean L\* of frames 1–8, the end is the mean L\* over [T − 10, T) s after the opening (16 fps).
- A scene's value: the mean of α over all seeds of that scene.
- No collapse exclusion rule (as in preregistration 10).

## Hypotheses (`analyze_s20.py`, frozen; Holm correction, 3 tests in total; T = 60 s)
- **H1 the sink keeps a share:** α(sf60_s3) − α(sf60_s0) > 0.
- **H2 re-rotation lowers what is kept:** α(sf60_s3rr) − α(sf60_s3) < 0.
- **H3 the re-rotated sink still keeps more than no sink:** α(sf60_s3rr) − α(sf60_s0) > 0.
- **Test method:**
  - two-sided one-sample t-tests over scenes;
  - supported if Holm p < 0.05 and the estimate is on the predicted side;
  - 95% CIs by scene bootstrap (10,000 draws).

## Secondary analyses
- H1–H3 in the [40, 50) s window;
- α of each condition at 10, 20, 30, 40, 50, 60 s;
- H1–H3 estimates for each seed separately;
- variance decomposition of α at 60 s: between scenes and between seeds (one-way random-effects ICC);
- the 31→34 s jump of each condition: α([34, 35)) − α([31, 32));
- side by side with the seed-0 results for the same scenes and conditions in preregistrations 10, 16 (descriptive).

## Predictions
- **H1 holds:** about +0.5.
- **H2 holds:** about −0.4.
- **H3 holds:** about +0.15 (re-rotated sink about 0.2, no sink about 0.05).
- **Jump:** the sink at its original positions shows the 31→34 s jump with all three seeds; the re-rotated sink and no sink do not.
